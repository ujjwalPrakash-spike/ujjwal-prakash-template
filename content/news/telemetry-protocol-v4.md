During recent high-velocity aerodynamic testing at Paul Ricard, the engineering team deployed Version 4 of the chassis telemetry protocol.

Integrating 48 strain gauges directly into the autoclave-cured front splitter, the system relays dynamic downforce shifts to the race engineer's console in real time.
